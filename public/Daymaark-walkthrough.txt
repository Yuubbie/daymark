DAYMAARK — HOW TO USE IT
========================

App: https://daymark-eosin-five.vercel.app
Press Ctrl+Shift+R once so you are not on an old page.

This paper is for walking someone through Daymaark.
Read it out loud if you want. One step at a time.


WHAT THIS APP IS
----------------
A school uses Daymaark to see what happened today.

The office runs the school.
The teacher marks who came, and writes what was taught.
The parent sees that same day on their phone.
A test the class sat becomes a mark on one sheet.
That sheet is the term card.

If fees are not paid, the parent cannot open the term card.
That is on purpose.

Daymaark does not do hostel, bus, library, pay, or bulk SMS.

PART 1 — START A SCHOOL
-----------------------
Do this on a new account. Skip this part if you are using Greenfield.

1. Open the app.
2. Tap Create account.
3. Type your name, email, and a password.
4. If it asks you to open an email, open it.
5. You land on a welcome page.
6. Tap I run a school.
7. Type the school name.
8. Type your name.
9. Add a crest if you have one. This picture becomes the school on
   every page and every print. You can add it later under Account.
10. Tap create.
11. You are now the proprietor. You own the school.
    You land on Today.

Do these four jobs, in this order. Today shows you which ones are still open.

Job 1. People
   Tap People.
   Invite an admin. They run the day. You still own the school.
   Invite a teacher when you are ready.

Job 2. Set the term
   Tap Set term.
   Example: First Term 2026/2027.
   Put start date and end date.
   The register and the term card use this term.

Job 3. Classes
   Tap Classes.
   Tap New class.
   Name it the way the school does. Example: JSS 1A.

Job 4. Children
   Open the class.
   Tap Add.
   Paste a list of names, or type them.
   Put admission numbers if the school has them.

On each child you can:

   Code
     Makes a short code. Give it to the parent.
     The parent types that code to see their child.

   Codes
     Makes a code for every child in the class at once.

   Login
     Invite the child's own email, if the child will sit a test
     with their own sign-in.

   Photo
     Put the child's picture. It prints on the ID.

   Fees
     Mark the child as paid, or fees due.

   Assign
     Put a teacher on this class. Form teacher, or by subject.

Also before a visitor walks in:

   Account
     Address and phone. They print on the back of the ID card.

   Fees
     Set what the class pays this term, in instalments.

   Apply
     People who want a place fill /apply/ then the school name slug.
     Their form lands here. Enrol puts the child on the class list.


PART 2 — THE TEACHER IN THE MORNING
-----------------------------------
Sign in as the teacher. You land on Register.

1. Pick the class if you teach more than one.
2. Tap All present.
3. Change the two or three who are not present.
     P = present
     L = late
     A = absent
     E = excused
4. Tap a name if you want one short note. Example: Came after assembly.
   The parent sees this note. Keep it to one line.
5. It saves by itself. If the phone has no network, it waits and
   sends later.

Then tap Lesson.

1. Pick the class and the subject.
2. Type the topic.
3. Type a short summary of what you taught.
4. Type homework if there is any, and when it is due.
5. Save.

One subject, one day, one form. That is all.

Timetable is the week.
Notices is a note to the class.


PART 3 — THE OFFICE TODAY
-------------------------
Sign in as proprietor or admin. You land on Today.

Today shows:
  how many children
  who marked the register
  who posted a lesson
  who is falling behind
  whose birthday is near

The list on the side:

  Today
  Register        you can mark it too
  Lesson          same form as the teacher
  Timetable
  Flagged         children whose attendance is falling
  Bank            questions for tests
  CBT             the papers
  Marking         mark and approve
  Classes         children, codes, IDs, reports
  People          staff
  Fees            what to pay, and remind one parent
  Apply           people asking for a place
  Notices         a note to the school, a class, parents, or staff
  Account         crest, address, phone

Inside a class:

  IDs       print student cards. Browser print. Card size is CR80.
  Reports   the term card for that child
  Codes     parent codes for the whole class


PART 4 — TESTS AND EXAMS
------------------------
A test in Daymaark is not a new book.
You write questions once. You put some of them on a paper.
The class sits it. You mark the written ones. The office says yes.
Then the parent can see the mark.

Step A. Bank

  1. Tap Bank.
  2. Tap New bank.
  3. Give it a name. Example: JSS 1 Mathematics.
  4. Pick the subject and the class.
  5. Tap Add question.

  Four kinds of question:

    Objective         A B C D. One right answer.
    Multiple select   More than one right answer.
    Subjective        The child writes. A person marks it.
    Comprehension     A passage, then small questions under it.

  Build the bank. Use it again next time.

Step B. Set a paper

  1. Tap CBT.
  2. Tap Set paper.
  3. Give it a title.
  4. Pick the kind:
       assessment  = a class test
       exam        = the term paper
       interview   = for a new child who wants a place
  5. Pick the class.
  6. Put how many minutes.
  7. Write short instructions.
  8. Tick questions from the bank.
  9. Save. It is a draft. Nobody can sit a draft.

Step C. Publish

  On the paper, tap Publish.
  Now the class can sit it.

  Then you get:

    Link
      Like a Google Form. Any phone. No Daymaark account.
      It looks like /sit/ then a short code.

    Poster
      For a class test or exam. Not for an interview.
      A page with a QR code for the classroom door.
      Press Poster as many times as you like. Same link.

    Interview
      For a new child. They type their name.

    Close
      When time is up.

Step D. Sit the paper (borrowed phone)

  Open the link, or scan the poster.

  1. Type the child's name. That is who they are. No login.
  2. Tap start. The clock starts now.
  3. Answer. Submit.

  Rules:
    One name, one try.
    Close the phone and come back with the same name = carry on.
    Already submitted = cannot start again.

  A child with their own login sits from Desk instead.

Step E. Mark and approve

  Tap Marking.

  The teacher marks written answers.
  The proprietor or admin taps Approve.
  Until Approve, the parent cannot see the score.

  Reject sends it back.

  The term card only shows papers that were approved.
  If the card has no marks, nothing has been approved yet.


PART 5 — THE PARENT
-------------------
The parent creates an account, or signs in.

Welcome page. Tap I am a parent.
Type the code the school gave them.
On Greenfield that code is ADAEZE01.
They can add a phone number.

Today shows:
  the child's name
  the attendance strip for the term
  today's lessons
  the teacher's one-line notes

Then:

  Homework    work still open
  Results     approved papers. Open card = the term card. Print from
              the browser if you want paper.
  Notices     school notes
  Fees        what is owed. If fees are due, the term card stays locked.
  Settings    phone number

One child, one sheet.
More than one child: tap the name at the top.


PART 6 — THE CHILD
------------------
The office invites the child's email from the class, under Login.

The child signs in and lands on Desk.
They see open tests and exams for their class.
They can open their report card.
Same fee rule as the parent.


PART 7 — SOMEONE WANTS A PLACE
------------------------------
No login.

Open /apply/ then the school slug.
Greenfield: /apply/greenfield-demo

The guardian fills the form.
The office sees it under Apply.
They can mark contacted, offered, enrol, or decline.
Enrol puts the child on a class list.
Then make a parent code as usual.

For an entrance test: publish an interview paper.
Give them the Interview link.
They type the child's name.
