Proprietor brief

How to open Daymaark for your school

You own the school. You appoint an administrator to run the day. Parents do not join until classes and the roster exist. Students sit tests on their own logins.

Open signup
  1. 01
    Proprietor

    Create the school

    Go to daymark-eosin-five.vercel.app/signup. Confirm the email, then on Welcome choose I run a school and name it. You become proprietor. You are not the day-to-day admin.

  2. 02
    Proprietor

    Appoint an administrator

    Open People and Invite admin. They sign up with that exact email. They run classes, fees, and the register. You keep the executive seat.

  3. 03
    Admin

    Set the term, then classes

    On the home board, set the current term. Attendance and reports hang off it. Then create classes — one per stream, for example JSS 1A.

  4. 04
    Admin

    Add students (the roster)

    Open a class and Add students. Paste names. This is the register, not a login. Parents and students still have their own doors.

  5. 05
    Admin or proprietor

    Invite teachers

    People → Invite teacher. They mark attendance, post lessons, and publish papers.

  6. 06
    Admin, then parent

    Hand parents their codes

    On the class page, generate claim codes and send them home. The parent signs up, chooses I'm a parent, and enters the eight-character code once. Do not invite parents before the roster exists.

  7. 07
    Admin, then student

    Give each student a login

    On the class page, open a student and tap Login. Enter their email. They sign up with that exact address and land on /student. When a teacher publishes a test or exam, they tap Start. This is not the parent claim code.

Money and lockout

  • 30 days free, then N200,000 a term for the whole school.
  • If a parent has not cleared fees, CBT and results stay locked for that family.
  • Teachers, parents, and students are not charged per seat.
