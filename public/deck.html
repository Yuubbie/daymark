Why a school switches.

Five people run a school day. They currently run it on paper, a spreadsheet and a WhatsApp group. Daymaark puts all five on one record — and shows the family the same day.

The parent

The term card

A report card that is not a new form anyone fills in December.

  • School, name, class, admission number, photo.
  • Attendance strip for the term, with the one-line notes under it.
  • Subjects rolled up from approved papers only — drafts never appear.
  • Prints on plain paper. One sheet per child, or the whole class at once.
  • Staff can print even when fees are due; the parent view stays gated.

Daymaark
Fees

Fee collection, and a gate the school actually controls.

  • Set what each class owes per term, split into instalments.
  • Parents see their own statement and can pay.
  • Mark a child fee-cleared and results unlock for that family.
  • Remind parents with a prefilled WhatsApp or SMS note — one name at a time.
  • Proprietor sees collection, outstanding and margin for the term.
The gate

If fees are outstanding, the report and CBT stay locked for that family. That is the collection lever working, not a bug.

Schools keep the choice: staff can still print term cards for the classroom even while a family is gated.

Daymaark
Identity & onboarding

Getting the right people in, with paper and a code.

Claim codes

An eight-character code per child, printed as a slip. The parent signs up and enters it once.

Identity cards

CR80 credit-card size, front and back as a pair. Cut, fold, laminate. The school's crest sits on top.

Student logins

Not the parent code. The school invites the student's email; they sign up and sit exams on their own desk.

A name on the class list is not a login. Daymaark keeps the roster, the parent link and the student login as three separate, deliberate steps.

Daymaark
Admissions

A public apply link that becomes a seat in a class.

  • The school gets a public form at its own link — no login for the parent.
  • Enquiries land in the office with the contact the guardian gave.
  • One tap enrols the child onto a class.
  • The child appears on the roster, ready for a claim code and an ID card.
Why it matters

Intake, roster and parent onboarding become one continuous flow instead of a paper application that gets re-typed into the register a week later.

Marketing and login stay Daymaark. The apply page greets the family with the school's own name.

Daymaark
It is your school, not ours

Upload the crest once. Every page becomes the school's.

  • Add the crest while naming the school, or later under Account.
  • It replaces the Daymaark mark on the dashboard.
  • It sits on ID cards, claim slips and term cards.
  • No crest? The school's name stands in its place.
Stays Daymaark

Only the marketing site, login and signup stay Daymaark. Everything inside the school wears the school's identity.

One platform can serve thousands of schools and still feel like each one's own system.

Security & data

Scoped by school. Then by family.

  • Access is enforced at the database, not just the screen.
  • Staff see their school. Parents see their own children only.
  • Another school's data is never visible, ever.
  • Students see their own record, not the class's.
The test that matters

Create two schools and two parents. Confirm a parent sees only their child, and nothing from the other school.

Same-school leakage between families is the failure we treat as release-blocking.
