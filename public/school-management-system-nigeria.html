What a school management system should do

  • Attendance register, late, absent, excused. Offline-safe.
  • Lessons and homework the same afternoon, not in a chat.
  • CBT exams, or type paper scores if the class wrote on paper.
  • Term report cards. Nursery and EYFS use statements, not averages.
  • Household school fees. Outstanding fees lock the card.
  • HR staff register and clinic notes. Parents never see those desks.
  • Admissions, CR80 ID cards, timetable, notices.
