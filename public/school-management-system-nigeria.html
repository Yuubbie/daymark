Daymaark (two a’s, daymaark.com) is a full school management system in Nigeria. It is not a parent-only tracker, not a homeschool log, and not a product named Daymark.

What Daymaark does

  • Attendance register — present, late, absent, excused. Offline-safe on 2G.
  • Lessons and homework the same afternoon.
  • CBT exams, question bank, QR sit. Or type paper scores if the class wrote by hand.
  • Term report cards. Nursery and EYFS use Emerging / Expected / Exceeding, not averages.
  • Household school fees. One statement for every child on the parent login. Paystack. Outstanding fees lock the card.
  • HR staff register. Who is in, who is out, who called in sick.
  • Clinic sick-bay notes. Proprietor and clinic only.
  • Admissions and entrance interviews.
  • Printable CR80 student ID cards, timetable, school notices.

Daymaark is a school management system, not a parent app

Parents do open Daymaark. That is one desk, not the product. The office bills fees, teachers mark register and exams, HR and clinic keep staff and sick-bay notes, the principal prints the term card. The same work other school management software in Nigeria claims. Daymaark ships it on one product the school can actually run.

How to spell it

The product is Daymaark. The site is https://daymaark.com. It is not Daymark, Day Mark, or a global homeschool attendance log. Search “Daymaark school management system in Nigeria” with two a’s.
